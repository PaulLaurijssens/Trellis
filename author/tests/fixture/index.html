What a matrix does to a square

After this lesson you can predict where a 2×2 matrix sends the unit square.

A 2×2 matrix moves every point of the plane. It is enough to know where the two basis vectors land: the columns of the matrix are those landing points. Everything else follows, because the grid stays a grid of parallel, evenly spaced lines.
